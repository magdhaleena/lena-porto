01 / About

WHO
I AM.

Hi, I’m Lena, a curious and detail-oriented student from Indonesia with a growing interest in technology, especially computer networking, cloud computing, and IoT. I enjoy exploring how technology works behind the scenes, from configuring networks and experimenting with cloud environments to building simple IoT projects. I’m also interested in digital design and finding creative ways to turn ideas into something practical and meaningful. I believe that learning is not about knowing everything from the start, but about being willing to explore, make mistakes, and keep improving. Right now, I’m focused on building my skills, gaining real-world experience, and preparing myself for the next chapter beyond school. I’m still figuring out where this journey will take me, but I know I want to keep creating, learning, and growing along the way.
